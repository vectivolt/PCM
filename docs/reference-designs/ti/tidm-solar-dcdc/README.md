# TIDM-SOLAR-DCDC - MPPT DC/DC: two-phase interleaved boost + isolated LLC (TI)
Digital MPPT front end on C2000. TI's product page states the design is replaced by TIDA-010938 (a 10 kW GaN string inverter with storage port; not fetched).
- **Native ratings** (TIDU404 section 1.2, Sept 2014): panel 200-300 V; 400 V DC out; 500 W; full-load efficiency > 94 %; boost 100 kHz (two phases 180 deg apart), LLC 120 kHz; Piccolo F2803x control card, 12 V bias.
- **Informs:** MPPT algorithm and input-voltage-reference loop (PV-02, PV-19); two-phase interleave scheme (PV-21).
- **Limitation (roadmap):** 500 W, 200-300 V to 400 V learning platform, superseded. The LLC stage is irrelevant for the non-isolated PV-P75.
- **Doc mismatch:** TI's page names an F280049C controller, but the only PDFs TI links for this tool are the 2013/2014 Piccolo-era guides (TIDU404, SPRABR6); neither mentions F280049C or "TIDM-SOLAR-DCDC" (grep-checked). Newer docs are HTML inside the C2000Ware DigitalPower SDK (not fetched).
- **Files:** TIDU404 user's guide, SPRABR6 application report (extra); TI publishes the schematic and BOM of this tool only as ZIPs: `tidm-solar-dcdc_schematic_tidr756.zip` (TIDR756A: one binary CAD .sch plus a TI notice, no PDF schematic, kept packed) and `tidm-solar-dcdc_bom_tidr758.zip` (TIDR758A; the two xlsx BOMs are unpacked in `design-files/tidr758/`: master BOM Rev 4 of 25 Jan 2021 and a REACH/RoHS BOM). Parts from the master BOM: boost 2 x IPP60R250CP (650 V) with C3D10060G diodes and 500 uH CTX16-18833-R inductors, UCC27324 gate drivers; LLC 2 x SPP20N60C3 with VITEC AF5457R transformer (1:1:1, 120 kHz) and AF5458R 55 uH inductor; AMC3330 isolated amplifier; HSEC8-160 / 100-pin DIMM control-card sockets. Not fetched: PCB layout TIDR757A, Gerbers TIDC475A, CAD symbols TIDR759.
