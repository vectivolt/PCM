# CRD250DA12E-XM3 - three-phase SiC inverter stack, liquid cooled (Wolfspeed)
Guide PRD-06975 Rev 5 (Jan 2024) covers CRD300 / CRD250 / CRD200DA12E-XM3 (modules CAB450 / CAB425 / CAB400M12XM3, XM3 1200 V half-bridge) on one cold plate with laminated bus, gate drivers, controller and sensors.
- **Facts from the guide:** DC bus 800 V nominal, 900 V max; 3 x 100 uF / 1100 V film capacitors (300 uF); power-loop inductance ~12 nH (5.3 nH bus + 6.7 nH module); LEM LF 510-S 500 A phase sensors; bus-discharge network 800 V to < 50 V in < 1 min; 50/50 water-glycol coolant, output derated above 25 degC coolant; highest test shown is 360 A rms at 800 V on the **CRD300** variant.
- **Variants** (design-files `Read Me.txt`): the documentation was released for the CRD300DA12E-XM3 and also supports the CRD250 and CRD200; they differ only in the module fitted: CRD300 = 3 x CAB450M12XM3, **CRD250 = 3 x CAB425M12XM3**, CRD200 = 3 x CAB400M12XM3. The per-variant rms current rating is still not stated in the guide or the design files; the "300 ARMS" in Wolfspeed's CRD250 page headline stays unverified.
- **Hardware shown by the design files** (documents unpacked in `design-files/`):
  - **Assembly BOM REV1** (for P/N CRD300DA12E-XM3): 3 x Fischer & Tausche CX100u1100d51KF6 DC-link capacitors (100 uF / 1100 V, as in the guide); Wieland Microcool CP3012-XM3 cold plate; 3 x LEM LF 510-S; Rogers laminated-busbar kit (DC link BB2522-002-00, output bars); 3 x CGD12HBXMP XM3 evaluation gate drivers; XM3 Controller V1.1; discharge PCB; two small fans (Delta AFB0512MB, EFB0412VHD). The gate-driver files are not in the archive ("available separately", `CHANGELOG.txt`).
  - **Controller V1R2** (8-page schematic, BOM, silkscreens): a TI LAUNCHXL-F28379D LaunchPad (UT13) is the processor; ISO1050 CAN, AM26LV31 / AM26LV32 RS-422 line drivers and receivers, SN74LVC1G123 one-shots, LT3581 regulators, LTC4368 surge stopper, RECOM R-78E5.0 / R-78E3.3 / R1SE-0505 supplies, AD8615 amplifiers.
  - **Discharge PCB V1** (1-page schematic, BOM R2): the schematic shows a 3 x 3 array of **68 kohm** 2 W 2512 resistors (CRGP2512F68K, 500 V working) across the 300 uF bank and states "discharge from 800 V to 50 V in under 1 minute" (our arithmetic: 68 kohm x 300 uF = 20 s, ln16 x 20 s = 57 s, consistent). The BOM lists nine **330 kohm** resistors (CRGP2512F330K), which would take about 4.6 minutes. The two documents disagree.
- **Informs:** GDRV/DESAT behaviour, busbar inductance and discharge-board lessons, sensing benchmark (ECO-05, ECO-07, ECO-09). Benchmark only.
- **Limitation (roadmap):** 900 V max and liquid cooling vs our 950 V forced-air target - not the production architecture.
- **Files:** user guide PRD-06975; `crd250da12e-xm3_design-files.zip` (57 MB; Allegro .brd, Gerber zips, STEP and AP203 models stay inside it); in `design-files/`: Controller (schematic, BOM, top/bottom silkscreen PDFs), Discharge PCB (schematic, BOM, silkscreen), Reference Design (assembly BOM), `Read Me.txt`, `CHANGELOG.txt`.
