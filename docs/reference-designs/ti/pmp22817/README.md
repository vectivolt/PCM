# PMP22817 - automotive SPI-programmable gate driver and bias supply, UCC5870-Q1 + UCC14240-Q1 (TI)
One-channel 800 V traction driver: UCC5870-Q1 (30 A peak, SPI-programmable protection, DESAT, CLAMP, VCE active clamp, ASC, six analog inputs) with a UCC14240-Q1 isolated bias module (+15/-5 V) and an LM5156-Q1 SEPIC making 24 V from a 6-42 V battery. TI: design not warranted production-worthy (schematic notice).
- **Native ratings** (TIDT256A Rev A Mar 2022 p.1, p.5, p.14, p.20; TIDMA63 Rev B p.1): bias and driver isolation 3 kVrms for 1 min, stated as sufficient for 800 V DC buses; +15/-5 V (69.8 kohm/10.0 kohm, 10.0 kohm/10.0 kohm); gate R 3 x 4.3 ohm per side; SEPIC 24 V / 130 mA at 445 kHz or 2 MHz; bias 1.6 W at 40 kHz into 100 nF -> UCC14240 73 degC at 21-23 degC ambient, no fan.
- **Informs:** GDRV bias module and barrier (D-016, UCC15241-Q1 option), bias start-up and self-heating references for the gen/gdrv.py design check, ECO-05 protection set (ASC and active clamp rejected for a DC/DC).
- **Limitation:** testing is about the SEPIC; the report defers UCC14240/UCC5870 performance to their datasheets (p.5) - no DESAT, fault or power-device test. The drawn active clamp (2 x SMCJ300A, 300 V stand-off each) cannot sit on the claimed 800 V bus; the BOM gives the UCC14240 as > 2.5 kVrms against 3 kVrms on p.1; p.5 misnames the bias part UCC14020-Q1. SPI driver is TI-only (D-031).
- **What we take from it:** +0.5 V start-up overshoot of the bias output (p.19) and about 32 K/W bias self-heating (p.14) for our design checks (detail in docs/requirements/REFERENCE-LESSONS.md).
- **Files:** test report TIDT256A, schematic TIDMA63 (Rev B), BOM TIDMA64 (Rev B). Not fetched: assembly drawing, PCB layout, Gerber/CAD (layout out of scope).
