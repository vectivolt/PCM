## 3. Cooling

- Heatsink pressure drop (per cell, geometry of sim/pv_tradeoff.py HS_GEOM): Shah & London apparent friction for developing laminar flow between parallel plates plus Kays & London entrance/exit losses: 35 / 60 / 89 Pa at 100 / 150 / 200 m3/h. Rest of the module: K = 10.0 on the duct velocity (ESTIMATE).
- Arrangement (assumption for this estimate; mechanics are out of scope): one 120 mm fan per cell in a common rear exhaust plenum. A dead fan leaks backwards (K = 4.0 stopped rotor; 200.0 with a backflow shutter).

| fan | full power at 45 C (fans 100 %) | noise at full power, 45 C (law) [dB(A) 1 m] | flow per cell [m3/h] | 1 fan failed, 45 C: shutter / none | current OK (<= 2.4 A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PFC1224DE-F00 | 100 % | 65.0 (speed 65 %) | 134 | 100 % / 100 % of P_max | yes |
| 4414-2HHP | 100 % | 58.1 (speed 81 %) | 105 | 100 % / 100 % of P_max | yes |
| 4414FNH | 100 % | 59.1 (speed 81 %) | 106 | 100 % / 100 % of P_max | yes |
| 9GT1224P1S001 | 100 % | 59.0 (speed 77 %) | 141 | 100 % / 100 % of P_max | yes |

**Choice: 9GT1224P1S001** (Sanyo Denki), one per cell: the quietest fan that holds full power at 45 C within the channel current. Noise basis: SPL 58 dB(A) at 1 m from the inlet at 100 % duty, 41 dB(A) at 35 % (p.484); +50 log10(speed) and +3.0 dB installation (ESTIMATE); fans summed incoherently.
- Rated -40..85 C ambient: PV-20's -30 C is met. Speed never below 52 % (its lowest specified PWM duty); capped at PV-P75 97 % (65.0 dB(A), 65.8 at 100 %), PV-P100/110 92 % (65.0 dB(A), 67.0 at 100 %) so that all fans stay <= 65 dB(A) at 1 m; derating below is computed at that cap.
- Fan law: speed = max(heatsink ramp 30 % at 40 C -> 100 % at 60 C, inductor ramp 30 % at 90 C -> 100 % at 115 C). A heatsink-only law let the inductor reach ~128 C at 35 C inlet / full load (fans ~50 %): the inductor NTC (roadmap: inductor temperature sensing) must take part. Fan power ~ speed^3.
