Three independent protections against a discharge into a live source:

1. **Hardware inhibit while the main contactor is closed** (INT-01): each port's TPSI3050 has its own EN, fed by K_DISCH OR FB_x_MAIN through two BAS16. FB is +24V through 1.5 k whenever the mirror contact is open (contactor closed - also welded), so an E-stop, a K_DISCH wire break or a SYS DO5 failure cannot start the discharge of a closed port: EN stays >= 11.6 V (needs 6.5 V). FB keeps an already-running TPSI3050 on (1.9 mA); it cannot start one (27 mA start-up) - SYS energises K_DISCH first at every power-up, so SYS may now cut K_DISCH unconditionally.
2. **Time limit** on the HV side (works without any 24 V): DIS_G charges 1.0 uF through 4.7 M; a MMBT3906 compares it with DIS_G/2 and fires a MMBT3906/MMBT3904 latch that clamps the gate to ~0.75 V (SiC VGS(th) >= 1.6 V hot). Limit **2.39-5.07 s** (zener at low current, divider, VBE + diode, leakage, X7R -35/+10 %) vs the longest needed discharge 1.77 s (1 mF envelope, R_dis +5 %). Latch holding: 23.2 uA available at 250 V vs ~1.3 uA needed; reset when K_DISCH re-energises (BSS138 shorts DIS_G).
3. **Thermal cut-off**: Honeywell 3100U00031461 (closes on rise at 82 +/-3 C, reopens at 60-70 C) on one RST 200 housing, wired DIS_G -> 100 k -> latch trigger (132 uA): it fires the same latch, so it acts on the HV side too. It closes after 3.6-6.0 kJ into a housing that starts at 60 C (A_CTH_RST, adiabatic) = 4.1-6.8 s of live-source discharge: it stops repeated activations (e.g. an intermittent K_DISCH) that the per-event time limit allows. It sits at HV: mount it on the housing over an insulating pad giving **basic HV-PE insulation (1000 V DC working, OVC II)**; its contact carries uA (no dry-circuit rating published).

## 4 Voltage channels (VA, VAX, VB, VBX, AUX1, AUX2): divider + AMC3330

6 x TNPV1206 1.00 M + 4.99 k: 1 V = 1203 V, clip 1504 V; 167 V and 27.8 mW per element at 1000 V.

**Input filter 4.7 nF C0G 5 % (was 10 nF)** on every divider (VA, VAX, VB, VBX and the IMD AUX1/AUX2): R_th 4.986 k -> tau 23.4 us (6.79 kHz). Voltage-sensing lag = tau (+5 %) + AMC3330 delay (2.6 us max, 50-90 %) = **27.2 us** (was 52.5 us; <= 30 us asked by the control simulation for the frozen-controller over-voltage case). Ramp lag error at 0.5 V/us: 11.7 V (was 24.9 V). TI's rule (filter corner >= 10x below the 20 MHz modulator clock) holds. ### 6.2 Hold-closed interlock (hardware rule)

Rule: above the contactor's breaking capability (450 A at 1000 V, once) the contactor must not open until the current is cleared. Each port has **two independent channels** (own REF3030E, own 0.1 % network, own TLV3502, OR and AND gate) on the shared IA/IB AMC3302 outputs. Network per side: +IN = 49.9 k from OUTP, 118 k to AGND, 86.6 k from the proof-test driver (118 k || 86.6 k = 49.9 k keeps the common-mode cancellation); -IN = 49.9 k from OUTN, 88.7 k to REF, 113 k to AGND. HOLD = OC AND FB_x_MAIN (closed) AND DIAG (valid). Channel 1 drives the IRFR9214 high side from the port +24V, channel 2 the IRFL214 low side (sec. 7). Trip current 410 A nominal, worst-case band **382-441 A** per channel (shunt 5 %, AMC gain/offset, REF, resistors, comparator offset + hysteresis): below 450 A and above every OC trip (202 / 270 A). The hold needs BOTH channels (2oo2); a disagreement near the threshold opens the contactor below 450 A.

E-stop during a fault: both safety channels cut the contactor supply (DO stage), not the port +24V, so the gates stop at once and the hold path keeps the coil energised while |I| is above the threshold; the contactor opens at zero current after the fuse has cleared. The hold cannot close an open contactor (needs FB = closed) and drops out on a sensor fault (DIAG). Longest hold = fuse clearing at the low end of the band: 1498 s (battery-port 200 A gBat, -10 % curve). Residual: loss of ALL 24 V with > 450 A (see 6.6); a stuck AMC3302 output with DIAG valid disables both channels and the CTRL SC trip together (common sensor).

### 6.3 / 6.4 PV-class fuse against a stiff source (reference only)

A gPV link sized for heat and a single HVC43 leave the battery-fed band open (135 A: none; 180 A: gap 384-19998 A). That is why a port that faces a battery or a stiff DC bus must be built with source="battery" (6.7): it is not a permitted configuration of a PV-class port.

### 6.5 PV source (current-limited)

A PV array cannot exceed about A_ISC_PV x rated current at the module terminals: 169 A (135 A port) / 225 A (180 A port). Both are below the hold band and below 450 A, so the main contactor always breaks a PV-fed fault (OC trip or E-stop). The port fuse never clears a PV-fed fault (below its 1.13 In non-fusing current: 181 / 282 A) and is not meant to: it protects the module's cabling against reverse current from the module side (bus capacitors, port B). Array-side faults are the job of the string fuses in the PV combiner (IEC 62548).

### 6.7 Battery-type port (port(..., source="battery")): ETI gBat + HVC43 pair + Raycap SPD

A battery rack or stiff DC bus. **Prospective short-circuit current: 20 kA assumed, <= 30 kA allowed at L/R <= 1 ms** (the ETI 1000 V gBat breaking capacity; was <= 50 kA with the gPV link). Port fuse: ETI NH gBat 1000 V d.c. (IEC 60269-7). Contactors: **two HVC43-250A in parallel**, coils in parallel on K_x_MAIN, one S10K30 per coil, each low-side switch doubled. Worst branch carries 0.662 of the port current (HVC43 contact resistance 0.06-0.25 mOhm, A_R_BRANCH 0.20 mOhm +/-10 %). The last contactor of the pair to open breaks alone, so the hold band stays 382-441 A.

### 7.4 Proof test of the hold (PA-04)

Input: DO_SPARE (DO8) = TEST, 100 k / 15 k -> SN74LVC1G17 per port. Four injection signals (each into +IN through 86.6 k): T -> channel 1 side A; T_d (47 k / 22 uF delay) -> channel 1 side B; one-shot of T (3.3 uF / 47 k) -> channel 2 side A; one-shot of T_d -> channel 2 side B. Readback (spare line of an existing connector): per port RB1 = OC1 OR hold-high-side-output, RB2 = OC2. ### 7.5 Precharge-complete criterion (INT-19)

VAX - VA after the 1-point calibration alone: +/-12.8 V at 1000 V (raw), inrush up to 220 A. Firmware nulls the VA/VAX (VB/VBX) gain mismatch whenever the main contactor is closed with |I| < OC and stores it; the residual is the drift until the next precharge (A_DT_NULL = 20 K: TCR 50 ppm/K, AMC gain 45 ppm/K, nonlinearity, offset drift): **+/-4.4 V worst / 3.1 V RSS at 1000 V, +/-1.24 V at 250 V**. Real |dV| at completion <= 14.4 V -> inrush <= 139 A (TDK make test 140 A). Before a null exists (first start, sensor exchange) firmware completes only on settling: VA changes < 1 V over one tau_max (220 ms); the physical difference is then <= the bleeder current x R_pre (0.5 V) + 1.6 V.

## 8 Insulation-resistance measurement (PV-C5)

Each test switch is a **common-source pair of C2M1000170D** driven by one TPSI3050 (SLVSFJ7D two-wire AC switch, PA-09): it blocks both polarities, so a pole below PE (dark PV array with port B live) no longer leaks through a body diode into the 'open' string.

R_t = 8 x 124 k = 992 k per switch; AUX dividers 6.005 M. State P: (V1 - u1)(G_ip + G_a + G_t) = u1 (G_in + G_a); state N: (V2 - u2)(G_ip + G_a) = u2 (G_in + G_a + G_t); R_iso = 1/(G_ip + G_in). Swing u1-u2: 33 k -> 8.0 V at 250 V; 1 M -> 108 V. Worst error: 33 k 9.9 %, 1 M 2.0 % (IDSS typ) / 16 % (IDSS max -> EOL baseline). tau 7.1 s.

**Rev F0 (REFERENCE-LESSONS 1-5, IC-11)**: array capacitance sized at 200 nF/kWp: 16.5 uF (PV-P75) / 22.0 uF (PV-P110) -> tau 7.1 / 9.4 s at 1 MOhm; settle-and-wait 3 tau = 21 / 28 s per state (two states per result) - over the 10 s branch-on limit, so CTRL uses the 3-sample exponential prediction (samples 0.33 s apart, V_inf = (V0 V2 - V1^2)/(V0 - 2 V1 + V2), TIDUFG5): about 1.5 s per state, 3.0 s per result, C_iso as a by-product. **Open PE**: dividers return on PE_D (own M4 bond), test strings, MOV3 and Y capacitors on PE_T (removable strap, opened for the hipot); an open PE_T shows no swing between the states, an open PE_D shows AUX1 = -AUX2 = V/2 and no swing - a healthy system swings >= 2.5 V even at 10 kOhm (3 LSB), so CTRL flags 'IMD PE open' instead of reading infinite insulation (firmware rule, CTRL spec). **One active monitor per DC system**: the module disables its own by keeping IMD_SW_P/N de-energised (strings open, fail-safe); it still loads each pole with the 6.005 M dividers, which the active monitor must count (system spec). **IC-11**: the switch islands and TPSI3050 secondaries are HV nets; the PE-side SiC of each pair is the HV-PE crossing.
