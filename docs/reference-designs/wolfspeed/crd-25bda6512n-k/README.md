# CRD-25BDA6512N-K - 25 kW bidirectional three-phase T-type inverter / PFC (Wolfspeed)
One board, three T-type legs: C3M0032120K (1200 V, 32 mohm, TO-247-4) high and low, two C3M0025065K (650 V, 25 mohm, TO-247-4) in common drain as the middle switch; LC output filter; TI UCC5350MC drivers; TMS320F280039C on a TMDSCNCD280039C control card. All figures are Wolfspeed's: user guide PRD-08613 Rev. 2, Oct 2024 ("UG pN" = printed page), schematic rev 3.0, May 2024 ("sheet N" = Altium sheet number, x.1-x.3 = phases A-C), BOM rev 3.0 ("BOM line N" = its Line #). Nothing re-measured here; the firmware is not on file (UG p28: on request).
- **Informs:** the DC-to-AC product PCS-P125 (REQUIREMENTS section 8, AC-01..03; decision D-053) - the nearest published reference to it; cross-checked in `sim/pcs_crosscheck.py` -> `sim/out/pcs_design/crosscheck_wolfspeed.md`. Magnetics rows in `sim/data/reference_magnetics.csv` (REF-2, MAG-1).
- **Ratings** (UG Table 1 p10-11): DC 670 / 800 / 900 V (the table prints "Vrms" for DC - a typo); AC 380 / 400 / 480 V L-L (480 V derated at low line); 36 A rms at 800 V / 25 kW; 25 kW in both directions; 50 / 60 Hz; 60 kHz (programmable 47-60 kHz, p11); 320 x 235 x 50 mm, > 6 kW/L. Claimed: inverter 99 % peak at 40 % load and 98.5 % at full load (800 V, 400 V); PFC 99.25 % peak at 480 V, 98.5 % full load.
- **Tested conditions** (UG p32-41): inverter open loop (Lab 2) and closed current loop (Lab 3) from a DC source into a three-phase resistive load through an external Schaffner FN3256H-64-34 line filter (Fig. 26 p32), Yokogawa WT5000; PFC from a three-phase AC source into a DC load (Labs 5-7). the guide states that the unit was never tested grid-connected (p10); the grid-connected Lab 4 exists in the firmware, untested, gains not tuned (p33). The 15 V auxiliary (1.5 A) and the fans (12 V, 6 A total) come from external supplies (p30-31), so the measured efficiencies exclude drive, control and fan power. No reactive-power (low power factor) operation is described or tested.
- **Topology and devices** (UG 3.1 p12; sheet 2.x; BOM lines 55-56): T-type NPC, one device per position - 6 x C3M0032120K, 6 x C3M0025065K, 12 devices. Common-drain middle pair so that the high-side switch and one middle switch share the phase-node bias supply: 5 isolated bias supplies instead of 7 (UG p19-21). One heat sink per phase (Boyd 780453B extrusion, Max Clip MAX03NG), 1200 V and 650 V devices alternated for thermal coupling, 1 mm AlN insulators (UG 3.6 p15-16); the heat sinks are tied to the DC midpoint by 0 ohm links (sheet 2.x R25/R61). Wolfspeed's own loss simulation of SiC against a 24 kHz IGBT T-type: 275 W against 287 W at 25 kW, inverter mode (Table 2 p13).
- **Switching frequency and modulation:** 60 kHz; third-harmonic-injection PWM in inverter mode (UG p10); modulation index set by TINV_vdInvRef_pu in open loop (p33). Dead time and midpoint control are in the firmware, not on file.
- **DC link** (BOM lines 1-3, 61; UG p25; sheets 1 and 2.x): 8 x KEMET ALA8DD471EE500 (470 uF, 500 V snap-in aluminium electrolytic), 4 from DC+ to the midpoint and 4 from the midpoint to DC- = 1,880 uF per half, 75 uF per kW per half; the cans see 0.90 of their rating at the 900 V maximum. Per phase at the devices: 2 x TDK B32774D0205K000 (2 uF, 1100 V film, C9/C10) and 2 x KEMET CKC21C104 (0.1 uF, 650 V C0G 2220, C11/C12), one of each per half. 24 x 200 kohm 2010 resistors (R1-R24) across the bank for balance and discharge. Nothing else holds the midpoint: no balancing circuit; the modulation does it and a firmware limit trips it (Table 3).
- **Filter magnetics** (UG 3.4 p14-15, Fig. 3 p13, Fig. 5 p15; BOM lines 10, 46, 50, 63; sheets 1 and 2.x): LC filter, no grid-side inductor. Per phase a converter-side choke and 10 uF X2 film (TDK B32926C3106K000, 305 VAC, C22) in series with 0.68 ohm 7 W (TE SQMW7R68J, R26), in star; the star (YCAP) returns to the DC midpoint through C21 - 2.2 uF on sheet 1, "CAP FILM 0.1UF 440VAC" (WIMA MKX14W31005B00KSSD) in the BOM; the two disagree. Choke: BOM line 46 "Line Choke, 125uH, 100kHz, 6.5mOhm DCR" (L1_1..3, no maker, no part number); line 50 "Inductor, 131uH, 20kHz, 7.5mOhm DCR" (L5_1..3) is drawn in parallel with L1 on sheet 2.x, but the photograph (UG p28) shows one round-wire toroid per phase - we read L5 as an alternate footprint. The guide's procedure gave L_INV 102 uH and C_DM 24.9 uF (25 kW, 50 Hz, 64 kHz, 230 V); only 10 uF is fitted because the on-board filter is meant for nominal noise suppression, not EMI compliance - a separate EMI board was designed to IEC 61000-6-3 (details on request). Choke weight 700 g per phase at 64 kHz against 1,600 g for a 24 kHz IGBT design (Fig. 3). Constraints: height <= 40 mm, choke <= 120 C, minimum volume and loss, area-product method with powder-core design references (UG p15 and refs [8]-[9]). **Not given:** core, material, gap, turns, wire, loss (offered on request through the Wolfspeed forum). Bias magnetics: 5 x Wurth 750320528 push-pull transformers (interwinding capacitance about 9 pF, which the guide calls high for EMI and suggests an LLC bias supply for new designs, UG p20) and 5 x Wurth 744212820 82 uH common-mode chokes on the bias outputs (L3, L13, L14).
- **Gate drive** (UG 3.3 p14; sheets 2-8; BOM lines 12, 30-33, 48, 67-69, 87, 101-105): 12 x TI UCC5350MCDWVR (5 A / 5 A, active Miller clamp, UVLO, no desaturation input), one per device. PWM through SN74LVC126A buffers whose four output enables take PWM_EN (10 kohm pull-down: disabled by default; outputs pulled down), then 100 ohm / 330 pF at IN+. Bias per supply: SN6507 push-pull driver, 750320528 transformer, BAV70 rectifier, 82 uH common-mode choke, MMBT3904 with a 19 V zener as series regulator, a 3.3 V zener split -> +15 / -3.3 V (Fig. 1: "+15V/-3V"); 10 ohm + 1 uF filters per driver rail (the BOM lists these resistors as 4.7 ohm). Per device (sheet 2.x): turn-on 5.1 ohm (R40), turn-off 5.1 ohm in parallel with 5.1 ohm + SM5819 Schottky (R39, D3), 1 ohm in the Kelvin-source return (R41), 10 kohm + 1 nF gate-source at the pins, the clamp pin straight to the gate. **No desaturation or any other device-level short-circuit protection.**
- **Current and voltage sensing** (sheets 10, 12-16; BOM lines 106-109, 113): inductor current per phase by Allegro ACS733KLATR-65AB-T (isolated Hall, 1 MHz, 20 mV/A, 1.65 V at 0 A) between the choke and the filter capacitor, conditioned by an OPA4340 (U13); its FAULT output (threshold set on VOC by R123/R125) goes to the control card as Iocp. DC bus and midpoint through 6 x 1 Mohm strings each into an OPA2388 (U6); phase voltages through 6 x 1 Mohm 0.1 % strings per phase into an OPA4340 (U5); heat-sink temperature by an LMT87 per phase (NTC header option) and ambient by an LMT87-Q1; REF2033 for the 1.65 V offset. The current direction is calibrated in firmware per mode (UG p23).
- **Protection and where each trip lives** (UG 5.3 Table 3 p30; sheets 3, 11; BOM lines 45, 100): DC over-voltage > 900 V, midpoint over-voltage > 450 V, AC under-voltage < 50 V, phase over-current 40 A rms - all firmware limits (TINV_* variables); the over-current trip is one-shot and needs a reset. In hardware only: the ACS733 FAULT line (whether it reaches a trip-zone input depends on the firmware), driver UVLO, the PWM_EN buffer enable, and per phase a 40 A relay (American Zettler AZ2150-1A-12DEF) bypassing two 1 ohm NTC inrush limiters (Ametherm MS351R040) in series. No fuses, no DESAT, no discrete comparators.
- **Auxiliary supply** (UG p30-31, sheet 9): 15 V on J3 from a lab supply or from Wolfspeed's optional HV SiC auxiliary flyback board (PS1, fed from DC+ / DC-; not in the design files - the product page names a C2M1000170J in it); L7805 (5 V) and TLV1117 (3.3 V) linear regulators; relays and fan switches (2 x CSD18534) from 15 V; CAN through ISO1050, untested (UG p24).
- **Controller card** (UG 3.5 p15, sheet 15): TMS320F280039C on TI's TMDSCNCD280039C card in a 180-pin HSEC socket made of J13 (pins 1-120) and J14 (121-180); Fig. 1 still names F28379D. Code Composer Studio project on C2000Ware DigitalPower SDK 4.04, labs 2-7, no GUI; the guide points to TIDA-01606 for the firmware structure (UG p29). The card does PWM, the current loop (and the voltage loop and PLL in PFC mode) and every protection limit.
- **Measured efficiency** (UG Table 6 p42, Fig. 32 p43; Tables 8-10 p45-49): inverter 99.27 % peak (670 V, 7 kW), 99.00 % at 7-11 kW and 98.45 % at 24.85 kW (800 V / 400 V), 98.35 % at 25.98 kW (800 V / 380 V); at 900 V the source limited the test to 17.7 kW. PFC 99.28 % peak (480 V, 40 %), 98.51 % at 25 kW (400 V); THD 2.7 % and PF 0.999 at 25 kW, 20 % and 0.90 at 1.27 kW. The tables are not fully consistent: at full power Table 6's power columns imply 0.1-0.2 % less efficiency than its efficiency column, and Table 8's input power at 60-70 % load is lower than the output power.
- **Thermal** (UG 6.3 Table 12 p53): three fans of about 8 W, case temperature by thermocouple at 25 kW, 60 kHz: inverter 58.2 C (1200 V) and 55.5 C (650 V) MEASURED; junction 70.5 / 60.6 C CALCULATED from 28.5 / 11 W calculated losses and R_th(j-c) 0.44 / 0.46 K/W; PFC up to 65.3 C case / 72.4 C junction. Derating target 135 C; ambient not stated.
- **Stated limitations:** evaluation hardware, "not designed to meet any industrial, technical, or safety standards" (UG p3); not tested on the grid; nominal EMI filtering only; control card taller than the 40 mm height target (p15); CAN untested.
- **Files:** user guide; `crd-25bda6512n-k_design-files.zip` (21.6 MB: Altium project, SchDocs, PcbDoc, BomDoc and PCB layer PDFs - left inside the zip, no layout work in this project); in `design-files/` the schematic PDF and the BOM xlsx. The C3M0025065K data sheet was filed with this review (`docs/datasheets/power-semiconductors/`). See SOURCES.csv.

**What we take from it**
1. The per-device gate network for paralleled TO-247-4 parts: diode-steered asymmetric R_G, a 1 ohm Kelvin-return resistor, 10 kohm + 1 nF at the pins, the Miller clamp straight to the gate.
2. For any T-type variant, the common-drain middle switch: 5 bias supplies serve 12 channels.
3. The same per-unit filter choices as PCS-P125 - L for about 25 % peak-to-peak ripple of the peak current, C_f 2 % of base - and one published inductor mass (700 g for 125 uH / 36 A at 64 kHz) that calibrates our area-product cost check.
4. A measured efficiency to test our loss model on: devices plus choke DCR explain about two thirds of the measured loss at 25 kW; the rest, about 0.5 % of the power, is filter, relay, PCB and capacitor loss that a budget must carry.
5. What not to copy: every trip in firmware and no desaturation detection (D-050 keeps the discrete layer); 500 V electrolytics at 0.9 of rating; nominal EMI filtering; a midpoint bank that is only ever tested near unity power factor.
