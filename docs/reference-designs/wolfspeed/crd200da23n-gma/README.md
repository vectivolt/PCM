# CRD200DA23N-GMA - 200 kW three-phase two-level inverter for a 1500 V DC bus (Wolfspeed)
Three CAB5R0A23GM4T (2300 V, 5 mohm) WolfPACK half-bridge modules, one per phase; TI LAUNCHXL-F28379D controller; three CGD1700HB2M-UNA gate-driver boards (TI UCC21710, desaturation protection on every switch position, differential signalling); forced-air heat sink. All figures below are Wolfspeed's, from user guide PRD-09623 Rev. 2 (February 2026); nothing here is ours or re-measured.
- **Native ratings** (Table 1): 200 kW maximum, DC bus 1500 V typical, 167 A rms phase current, 20 kHz typical / 50 kHz maximum, junction-to-ambient 0.343 K/W.
- **Power stage** (section 3.1): DC link of two series-stacked sets of five KEMET C4AQQEW5650A3BJ film capacitors (65 uF, 1.1 kV each) = 162.5 uF at 2.2 kV, i.e. 0.81 uF per kW; the midpoint is brought out for a load neutral or a split supply; balance / bleed resistors permanently connected (below 50 V in under 2.5 minutes); six-layer interleaved DC+ / DC- planes (Table 8); an RC snubber at the module terminals (section 3.3, Figure 27: waveforms at 1500 V and 167 A with and without it).
- **Gate drive and protection** (section 3.2): 0 ohm external gate resistors on these boards (the standard driver board has 1 ohm); desaturation detection with soft turn-off. A tripped driver switches off only its own channel; the other channels are held off through the controller's trip-zone input, which the guide says needs the firmware to be set up for it (section 3.2.2).
- **Tested** (section 5.2, Tables 11-12): 1000 V bus (the supply is a 1000 V / 30 kW unit), 167 A rms, 20 kHz, 400 ns dead time, 25 C ambient, into a 129 uH / 600 A three-phase inductor in wye with its neutral on the capacitor midpoint - a reactive load, so the supply only covers the losses. Module thermistor 43 C after 30 minutes. Efficiency against output power is given as a curve only (Figure 50). A PLECS model is part of the design files.
- **Caveat in the guide** (page 1): laboratory evaluation hardware, "not designed to meet any industrial, technical, or safety standards", not production-qualified. No grid filter, contactors, EMI filter or grid protection on the board; the firmware was not reviewed here.
- **Firmware** (package v1.0.0, supplied by the owner on 2026-10-05, unpacked in `firmware/`; this replaces "the firmware was not reviewed here" above): an **open-loop starting point** — a sine-PWM generator with no current loop, PLL, DC-link loop or grid forming and no protection in firmware beyond the drivers' DESAT trip zone; read statically in [FIRMWARE-NOTES.md](FIRMWARE-NOTES.md), lessons in `docs/requirements/REFERENCE-LESSONS.md` §6 (D-071).
- **Informs:** the DC-to-AC product PCS-P125 (decision D-053). It is a published precedent for a two-level SiC stage with the devices at 0.65 of their rating (1500 V on 2300 V parts) in place of a three-level stage - the rule our 950 V on 1700 V parts (0.56) follows - and for the size of a two-level film DC link. It is also the voltage class of the price benchmark (Megarevo 1500 V string PCS).
- **Limitation:** power modules, not paralleled discretes; 1500 V class, not our 1000 V class; tested at 1000 V, not at the rated bus voltage with full current.
- **Files:** user guide (plain download from wolfspeed.com) and the design-files archive V1.1 (02-2026), supplied by the owner on 2026-10-05 and byte-identical to the file behind the product page's link. Unpacked in `design-files/`: the schematic PDF and the assembly BOM; the Altium and Gerber archives stay inside the zip (no layout work in this project). An older V1.0 (11-2024) package exists and is not filed. See SOURCES.csv.
