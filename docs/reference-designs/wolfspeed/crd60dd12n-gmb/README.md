# CRD60DD12N-GMB - 60 kW isolated bidirectional DC/DC, dual active bridge (Wolfspeed)
Two CBB011M12GM4T (1200 V / 11 mohm Gen-4 WolfPACK full-bridge) modules on one liquid cold plate, UCC21710 gate drivers, isolated DC-bus and transformer-current sensing, TI TMDSCNCD280039C controller with baseline firmware. No transformer supplied.
- **Native ratings** (PRD-10001 Rev 1, May 2026, Table 1): 60 kW max; Vin 700 / 800 / 900 V (min/typ/max); Vout 400 / 800 / 900 V, **power derated below 600 V**; Iout <= 100 A; 100 kHz fixed; coldplate-to-fluid 0.045 degC/W.
- **Transformer** (UG section 5.2, p51): recommended custom Egston/YAGEO 81989-01, 202 x 81 x 73 mm; L_leak 7.5 uH, L_m 329 uH, Rw 78 mohm at 100 kHz (Table 9). Turns ratio not published.
- **Validated range in the guide:** 800 V to 800 V, 10-23 kW only (source rated 30 kW). The 99.2 % / > 98.8 % figures are web-page claims, not stated in the PDF text.
- **Hardware shown by the design files** (V2.0 schematics and assembly BOMs, unpacked in `design-files/`; power board 10/2024 = 2 sheets, control board 02/2025 = 12 sheets; the control schematic prints reference designators without the BOM's T/B layer suffix):
  - **Modules:** Q1 (primary) and Q2 (secondary) CBB011M12GM4T, "1200 V 100 A", press-fit, not soldered (power sheet 1 "POWER STAGE"; power BOM DNP-27). The power board carries no gate drivers; J1 (Samtec TSM-128-01-L-DV-A) takes gate, Kelvin-source and sensor signals to the control board.
  - **Gate drivers:** 8 x UCC21710QDWRQ1, one per switch, 4 per bridge (control sheets 3-6 "DRIVERS (12P) / (34P) / (12S) / (34S)", two per sheet). Each has its own UCC14141QDWNRQ1 1.5 W isolated bias module fed from a +12 V rail, giving +15 V / -4 V (UG p33). Turn-on resistor 0.91 ohm (8 x RCWE1210R910FKEA, control BOM item 40), turn-off 0 ohm; the DESAT/OC input is sensed through two series US3M-13 diodes; the module NTC is read on the AIN pin of the switch-2 drivers only (sheets 3 and 5). Fault gating: SN74HCS21 4-input ANDs (sheets 7-8), SN74LV541A / TXU0104 level shifting (sheet 9).
  - **Controller:** TMDSCNCD280039C (F280039C, **not** F28388D) in an HSEC8-160 edge connector (sheet 1, DNP-90 in the control BOM). 12 V barrel-jack input through an LTC4368 surge stopper and 2 x DMT12H007LPS; 5 V and 3.3 V from RECOM R-78E converters; CAN through a TCAN1044V (sheets 1-2). No HV bootstrap supply and no 24 V rail.
  - **Transformer / inductor:** not in either BOM or schematic. The AC mid-points PA/PB (primary) and SA/SB (secondary) end in screw lugs (J2/J4/J6/J8, McMaster 4164N12); there is no series inductor and no DC-blocking capacitor on the board.
  - **DC link:** 5 x KEMET C4AQNEW5650M3BJ, 65 uF 5 % 1 kV film: C60, C61 on the primary (130 uF), C62-C64 on the secondary (195 uF) (power sheet 1; the MPN is confirmed by power BOM item 7). Bleed: 24 x 220 kohm 2 W (TE CRGP2512F220K, 12 per side), bus below 50 V in under 3 minutes (UG p28).
  - **HF capacitors:** 23 x KEMET LDEQG2470KA5N00, 47 nF 10 % 1 kV **film, 5040 case**: 10 on the primary link (C11-C13, C17-C23), 13 on the secondary (C27-C29, C33-C39, C43-C45) (power sheet 1; UG Fig. 19).
  - **Snubbers:** four circuits of 3 branches = 12 x (6.8 ohm 1.5 W 2512, Stackpole RPC2512JT6R80, + 2.2 nF 2 kV **X7R** 2220, KEMET C2220C222KGRAC7800), i.e. 6 per bridge: SNBP0/SNBP1 (R19-R21, R28-R30) and SNBS0/SNBS1 (R31-R33, R37-R39) (power sheet 1; UG Fig. 22).
  - **Sensing:** 4 x LEM HO 120-NP-0100 (primary DC at P-, primary AC at PB, secondary DC at S-, secondary AC at SA) with AD8616 buffers (power sheet 2; the S- channel is inverted in hardware, "invert in software"), AD8618 conditioning (control sheets 11-12); DC voltages by 2 x AMC3330QDWERQ1 behind 6 x 1 Mohm + 5.62 kohm dividers (sheet 10).
- **Checked against the project** (`sim/dab_devices.py`, `sim/out/dab_design/dab_spec.json`): module, gate driver, sensors, snubber network, DC-link banks and NTC read-out match; the bias module is the CRD's UCC14141-Q1 on 12 V, the project's UCC14241-Q1 on 24 V is a deliberate change (D-016). Three differences: the HF capacitors are film (5040), 10 + 13, whereas the project models 10 ceramic capacitors per bridge with ESR 10 mohm / ESL 1 nH (assumed); the fitted turn-on resistor is 0.91 ohm, whereas the guide (Table 11) and the project say 1 ohm; the CRD snubber capacitors are X7R, the project's own network uses C0G.
- **Informs:** DAB-D60 (S-3, DAB-01..09), transformer spec (DAB-06), GDRV (ECO-05).
- **Limitation (roadmap):** 900 V max port vs our 590-950 V DAB-10; operating map, transformer, controls and firmware are ours (firmware access terms unconfirmed).
- **Files:** user guide PRD-10001; `crd60dd12n-gmb_design-files.zip` (six nested ZIPs: cold plate, STEP, Altium, BOM, Gerber, Schematic; 65 MB); in `design-files/`: the schematic PDFs, assembly BOM xlsx, cold-plate drawings (PDF) and the two small cold-plate STEP files. No PLECS model and no firmware in the package.
