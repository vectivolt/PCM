### 0.4 Efficiency against DAB-04 (calibrated)

DAB-04 (99.2 % peak, > 98.8 % above 20 kW) is NOT MET. Claimed (calibrated to Wolfspeed's measured CRD loss, the model + 91 W + 0.0 W/kW): peak 99.10 %, 98.80 % at 800/800 V 60 kW, minimum 97.47 % above 20 kW in the full-power window (59 % of those points >= 98.8 %). Model alone (not claimed): peak 99.42 %, 98.95 % at 800/800 V 60 kW, minimum 97.91 %.

What the model lacked against Wolfspeed's CRD measurement (800/800 V, 10.8-22.7 kW, UG Fig. 57): the gap was 183-288 W. Already modelled before this round: dead-time body-diode conduction 7-20 W, HF-capacitor/snubber ring-down 2-7 W and the transformer copper loss at the measured 78 mOhm for the fundamental 15-69 W (so MG-01 does not by itself explain the CRD gap). Added this round: the harmonics of that copper loss, 91-239 W (our F_r(h) law applied to the measured R_w - an estimate): 73 % of the gap. Not modelled: C_oss hysteresis ~13 W (ASSUMPTION 10 % of E_oss per cycle), gate drive 10 W and sensing 1.5 W if Wolfspeed counted its 12 V input (with these 84 %). Residual 46-91 W is carried as a constant 91 W in the calibrated claim.

## 1. Summary

* **Turns ratio n = N1:N2 = 11:12 = 0.9167**, **total series inductance L = 6.50 uH** (transformer leakage target 0.94 uH + external 5.56 uH), **L_m = 150 uH** (gapped). Frozen by DECISIONS.md D-014. Re-check with the audited device data: the frozen choice costs +5.5 W mean loss (+1.26 %) against the current sweep optimum 12:13, 6.0 uH (continuous optimum n = 0.94, L = 6.0 uH), so D-014 stands.
* **Refinement round (2026-10-04):** device curves replaced by the independent audit's PDF vector-data values (E_on at 600 V was up to 21 % high, E_rr at 800 V up to 13 % high, C_oss ~3 % high), V_SD now a Fig. 7 table (the linear model was up to 11 % low at 25-50 A), switching SOA now the Fig. 19 polyline, transformer hot spot added as a derating limit (withdrawn 2026-10-04, MG-02). Pessimistic case, design levers and the one-branch-fault simulation added.
* **Model peak efficiency 99.42 %** at 600 V/650 V/+15 kW (target 99.2 %). Inside the full-power window, 27 of 190 feasible points at >= 20 kW fall below 98.8 % (minimum 97.91 %), listed in section 5.
* **Calibration warning:** the same model run with the CRD60DD12N-GMB configuration predicts 97.81-97.84 % at 10.8-22.7 kW, Wolfspeed measured 97.01-97.63 % (UG p55 Fig. 57). Measured loss is 1.1-1.4x the model. The 99.2 % / >98.8 % figures are web-page claims; the user guide only shows 97.0-97.6 %. If the unexplained CRD loss (91 W + 0.0 W/kW, fitted) is intrinsic, our 800 V/800 V efficiency becomes 98.47 % at 60 kW and 98.80 % at 30 kW: **neither target would be met**. Resolving this gap is the first bench task.
* **Worst junction temperature 90 C** at 600 V/650 V/-60 kW with 50 C coolant inlet (design ceiling 140 C, datasheet 150 C).
* **Pessimistic case (CRD gap 91 W + 0.0 W/kW added everywhere):** 800/800 V efficiency 98.47 % at 60 kW, 98.80 % at 30 kW. If the loss is in the modules: Tj up to 87 C, full power survives at 29 of 29 base full-power window points; fix: coolant inlet would have to be <= 50 C (impractical: below dew point in most sites); practical fixes: 3 x IV3Q12013T4Z / SG2M014120LJ per switch (Tj max 73 C at full power) or derate to the pessimistic map (60 kW kept at 29 of 29 base full-power window points). If it is in the transformer: hot spot 124 C, full power at 29 points; transformer hot-spot-to-coolant Rth <= 0.355 K/W would be needed (MG-02: 0.71 K/W is the best case of the reference pot core) - not credible: if the gap is in the magnetics the transformer must be redesigned for lower loss; otherwise derate per the pessimistic map. Section 5b.
* **950 V on 1200 V devices:** ACCEPTABLE WITH DERATING, cosmic-ray risk NOT quantified: (1) cosmic ray: neither IV3Q12013T4Z nor SG2M014120LJ has a published FIT-vs-DC-voltage curve; 950 V is 79 % of V_DSS. For scale only, Wolfspeed's curves give 0.9 FIT (Gen 4) to 51 FIT (Gen 3) per bridge at 950 V at sea level for 0.3 cm2 per switch - the makers' curve is a release condition. (2) switching SOA: no switching-SOA curve for the discretes; with the assumed 15 nH loop and t_f 15 ns the turn-off current at 950 V must stay below 137 A per switch position (V_DS,peak <= 1200 V); P_max at 950 V: 60.0 kW at V2=900 V, 52.5 kW at 800 V, 35.0 kW at 700 V. (3) the loop inductance is the critical assumption: verify by DPT.
* **Full-power window misses** (DAB-10 x DAB-11 at 60 kW, either direction): 600 V/850 V/+60 kW, 600 V/850 V/-60 kW, 600 V/900 V/+60 kW, 600 V/900 V/-60 kW, 900 V/700 V/+60 kW, 900 V/700 V/-60 kW, 900 V/750 V/+60 kW, 900 V/750 V/-60 kW. Root cause: switching SOA of the bridge on the 900 V side (turn-off current too high at that voltage). This challenges assumption DAB-10/11: either accept derating at the window corners, or run the PCS DC bus at V1 ~ n*V2 (then d ~ 1, ZVS everywhere).
* **ZVS:** with SPS, bridge 1 loses ZVS at light load when V1 < n*V2 and bridge 2 when V1 > n*V2 (`zvs_map_sps.png`). EPS/DPS/TPS from an offline LUT restores most of it and is required for light load and for the 950 V operating range; SPS alone is adequate only near V1 = n*V2 above ~30 % load.
* **DC flux walk:** open loop is not viable (a 5 ns pulse-width error gives ~44 A DC). Baseline: firmware flux-balance loop + gapped transformer + hardware trip; series blocking capacitor kept as a bolted-link provision.

## 2. Basis: what the reference design really is

* Module: **CBB011M12GM4T** (1200 V, 11 mOhm, full bridge, Gen 4, pre-applied TIM) on both bridges - UG p6, p8 sec 2.1; roadmap is correct.
* CRD Table 1 (UG p8): 60 kW, Vin 700/800/900 V, Vout 400/800/900 V, Iout <= 100 A, 100 kHz, coldplate-fluid 0.045 K/W per switch position; power derated below 600 V out (= 100 A limit). Our map reproduces this.
* Transformer: YAGEO/Egston 81989-01 (UG p51-52 Table 9): L_sigma 7.5 uH, L_m 329 uH, R_w 78 mOhm, R_c 1.24 ohm at 100 kHz, 202x81x73 mm, cold-plate cooled. **Turns ratio not published** (1:1 inferred from the 800 V/800 V test).
* Test (UG Table 11): 800 V -> 800 V, dead time 200 ns, RG(on) 1 ohm, RG(off) 0 ohm, 10-23 kW. The Table 11 phase column (2.5-5.0 deg) contradicts the guide itself: Fig. 55 shows 10.0 deg at 22.7 kW, which matches Table 9 (7.5 uH) and our SPS model (10.3 deg). Table 11 phase is not used and not combined with Table 9.
* CRD bulk capacitor C4AQNEW5650M3BJ (UG p23, "1 kV") is not in the C4AQ datasheet on file (no N voltage code or M release code); our calibration run uses the 1100 V sibling C4AQQEW5650A3BJ as a stand-in.
* Snubbers: 6 x (6.8 ohm + 2.2 nF 2 kV) RC across each DC link (UG Fig. 22); DPT simulation 800 V/100 A: 952 V peak without, 940 V with snubber (UG Fig. 21) = ~150 V overshoot.

* Datasheet inconsistencies found: (a) Fig. 8 C_oss is half of Fig. 9 at the same label voltage; it matches Fig. 9 if its axis is read x4 -> Fig. 9 + table used. ## 4. Power transfer, modulation, ZVS

SPS power P = n V1 V2 phi (pi - |phi|) / (2 pi^2 f L) (`power_vs_phase.png`). ZVS is evaluated per leg transition by integrating the inductor energy against the real C_oss(V) charge of the module (Fig. 9), a 150 pF node capacitance and the opposing bridge voltage. Incomplete transitions dissipate the residual capacitive energy; wrong-polarity transitions are hard switched (DPT E_on + E_rr). `zvs_map_sps.png` shows SPS; `efficiency_map.csv` gives the chosen a1/a2/phi and ZVS flags of the best modulation at every point.

**Dead-time phase drift (found by this comparison):** a bridge that does not complete its swing within the dead time changes polarity only when the incoming switch turns on, so its edge moves by up to one dead time (150 ns = 5.4 deg). Ports.** Authority = `sim/out/port_design/port_spec.json`: 220 ohm precharge, close at dV <= 10 V, HP10NH1GPV160 160 A gPV fuses, TDK HVC43-250A-24MC contactors with hold-closed interlock. dab_spec precharge values now copied from it (time to dV <= 10 V: 0.14 s port 1, 0.19 s port 2). dab_control starts the DAB after contactor closure with the bank 10 V below the battery - consistent.

**6. Transformer current sensor.** LEM HOB 130-P: +/-250 A, 1 MHz, t_D90 <= 200 ns (HOB-P p9) - meets the >= 1 MHz requirement; range is enough: peak operating 177 A, control transients <= 182 A with the split phase update, OC trip 210 A (200-220 A) = 88 % of range; required >= 242 A. A naive (unsplit) phase step would add ~47 A and reach the trip band at the corners - the split update is mandatory.

**7. Local OV trips** (board: port 1 972-1000 V, port 2 924-950 V, 63 us to the gates), load rejection with the full port current into the bank: port 1 0.76 V/us -> 1048 V peak, port 2 0.51 V/us -> 983 V peak. DC: inside the module 1200 V and both capacitor banks (port 1 1300 V class, port 2 1074 V allowed at its hot spot). Switching: while the bus is above ~950 V the turn-off SOA allows only 110 A (port 1 at 1048 V) vs up to 113 A at full load, so the firmware OV (960/920 V) must set the phase to zero within one switching period (cycle-by-cycle via CMPSS/trip zone) - the 63 us hardware path is the backup only.
