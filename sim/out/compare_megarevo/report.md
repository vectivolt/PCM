## Reading this table

- **BETTER / MEETS** compare a calculated figure of ours with a published figure of theirs. A calculated 99.5 % and a published 99 % are not the same kind of evidence.
- **BELOW** rows are the price of the cost-first build or open work, not rounding: each is listed with its remedy in `docs/guide/12-risks-and-open-items.md`.
- **NOT ASSESSED** rows need the mechanical design, which this project does not contain.
- Decisions and their open conditions are in `docs/requirements/DECISIONS.md`; the assumptions behind the module figures are in `sim/out/pv_design/module_report.md`.
