# PMP23223 - smart isolated gate driver with bias supply, UCC21732-Q1 + UCC14240-Q1 (TI)
One-channel isolated driver board for a SiC/IGBT switch or module: UCC21732-Q1 (10 A, DESAT on the OC pin, internal two-level turn-off, CLMPE external Miller-clamp FET, AIN/APWM temperature sense) fed by a UCC14240-Q1 isolated bias module from 21-27 V. TI: design not warranted production-worthy (schematic notice).
- **Native ratings** (TIDT283 May 2022 p.1, p.3, p.9-10; TIDMAI7 Rev A p.2; TIDMAI8 p.1): VIN 21-27 V / 500 mA; rails +18/-4 V (VDD-VEE 77.7 kohm/10.0 kohm, COM-VEE 13.2 kohm/22.0 kohm); > 1.5 W bias; 10 A source/sink; 2.2 ohm on and off; DESAT 5.4 V / 130 ns through 2 x STTH112A (disabled as shipped, R1 DNP); Si2318CDS clamp FET on CLMPE.
- **Informs:** GDRV (ECO-05): Miller hold of the paralleled PV gates (D-028), bias-rail programming (D-016, PV +20/-4 V), AIN/APWM path of the DAB module NTC; driver selection under D-031.
- **Limitation:** tested only into a 100 nF capacitor - no power device, no double-pulse, short-circuit or fault-timing data. Bench APWM points (80.33 % at 0.6 V, 40 % at 2.5 V, p.8-9) sit 8-10 duty points below the line TI quotes (88 % to 10 % over 0.6-4.5 V); p.10 states about 1.5 W at 35 kHz while its own equation gives 1.78 W; thermal ambient not stated.
- **What we take from it:** external N-FET Miller clamp from a CLMPE-type pin as the candidate fix for D-028, and a two-point AIN/APWM check before the DAB NTC trip is trusted (detail in docs/requirements/REFERENCE-LESSONS.md).
- **Files:** test report TIDT283, schematic TIDMAI7, BOM TIDMAI8. Not fetched: assembly drawing, PCB layout, Gerber/CAD (layout out of scope).
