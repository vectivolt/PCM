# TIDA-010985 - resistive-bridge insulation monitor for 800 V DC with large Y capacitance (TI)
Quasi-balanced bridge 500 k / 200 k / 500 k (24 x 200 k, 0.1 %) permanently across DC+/DC-, PE tied to either tap by two isolated solid-state relays (ratios 7:5 / 5:7); RES60A-Q1 12.5 M dividers + TLV9002-Q1 into an MSPM0G3507 LaunchPad on PE-referenced ground; 3-sample exponential prediction and Y-capacitance estimate; isolated CAN. TI: lab use only.
- **Native ratings** (TIDUFG5 Rev A, Tables 1-3/1-4, p.17): bus to 1000 V; R_iso tested to 2 M, +/-5 % (3 sigma); C_iso to 9 uF, +/-15 % (3 sigma); 2 s cycle, < 2 s at 4 uF / 1 M (the 1 M-1 M case needs a 3 s cycle); pole-PE swing <= 10 % of V_DC; 2 mA active / 100 uA idle leakage; bridge 0.85 W.
- **Informs:** PV-C5 IMD firmware (prediction, C_iso, simultaneous ratio sampling), bridge-topology option, IMD handling in the hipot (ECO-10), choice of the governing standard (ECO-11).
- **Limitation:** EV/ESS context (UL 2231-2, IEC 61851-23, thresholds 100 / 500 ohm/V); MCU ground tied to PE, not our reinforced HV-PELV scheme; guide Rev A says TPSI2240-Q1 (p.21) but schematic SLVRBZ5 and BOM SLURB83 Rev E2 fit TPSI2140-Q1; fixed-point MCU.
- **What we take from it:** 3-point exponential prediction + C_iso estimate for our 10 uF array, simultaneous ratio sampling, an IMD PE link that opens for the hipot (detail in docs/requirements/REFERENCE-LESSONS.md).
- **Files:** design guide TIDUFG5 Rev A, schematic SLVRBZ5, BOM SLURB83. Not fetched: example code (CCS/MSPM0), layer plots, assembly drawing, Gerber/CAD (layout out of scope).
