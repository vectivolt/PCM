# CRD-60DD12N - 60 kW four-phase interleaved boost converter (Wolfspeed)
Four 15 kW boost phases (C3M0075120K 1200 V 75 mohm TO-247-4 with C4D10120D SiC Schottky diodes; the guide also names E3M0075120K / E4D20120D variants) and two 12 V / 2.75 A fans. Analog control, no MCU.
- **Native ratings** (PRD-06979 Rev 2, Jan 2024, Table 1): Vin 470-800 V DC; Vout 850 V DC; **60 kW for Vin >= 600 V, 50 kW below 600 V**; 78 kHz; peak efficiency 99.5 %; 127 W/in3.
- **Hardware shown by the design files** (Rev 3 schematics, single sheet each, 18 Jan 2024; Rev 2 BOMs; documents unpacked in `design-files/`):
  - **Power board:** phases PH1H, PH1L, PH2H, PH2L, each = 2 paralleled C3M0075120K (Q1-Q8, 8 in total) + 2 C4D10120D (D1-D4, D25-D28), a 399 uH Cramer T26586 inductor (L1-L4) and a Pulse PE-67200NL current-sense transformer (CT1-CT4). Two separate PV inputs (+PV_1/-PV_1 on CON1/CON2 feed phases 1H/1L; +PV_2/-PV_2 on CON3/CON4 feed 2H/2L), each with a Cramer T46592 common-mode choke (XL1, XL2), 22 uF 630 V film (C1-C4) and 2.2 uF 630 V film (XC1-XC8). Output 2 x 100 uF 1 kV film (C5, C6, Vishay MKP1848C71010JY5). Four 60 A 1200 V rectifiers from each input rail to the output (D29-D32, DLA60I1200HA or VS-60EPF12PBF; our reading: start-up bypass). Gate drive: 4 x Wolfspeed CGD15SG00D2 gate-driver boards (XA1-XA4) from the 12 V rail. Bias: Wolfspeed CRD015DD17P 15 W flyback module (XA6), the 15 W sibling of CRD-020DD17P-J.
  - **Control board:** 2 x UCC28220D dual-channel interleaved PWM controllers (U201: phases 1H/1L, U203: phases 2H/2L), LM358 error amplifier (U202) and TL431 reference (U204); output and input voltage sense through resistor dividers. Everything is analog.
- **Informs:** PV cell layout and interleaving lessons (PV-19, PV-21): current sharing, phase shift, thermal.
- **Limitation (roadmap):** boost only, fixed 850 V output, 470-800 V input - not a 250-1000 V adaptive buck-boost; analog control, so no MPPT or digital-control lessons; the PV-P75 power stage is our own design.
- **Files:** PRD-06979 user guide; `crd-60dd12n_design-files.zip` (1.2 MB; Allegro .brd layouts, OrCAD .DSN/.opj schematic source and Gerber zips stay inside it); in `design-files/`: the two schematic PDFs and the two BOM xlsx (control and power).
