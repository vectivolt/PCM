# TIDA-011011 - isolated gate driver board with isolated bias supply for 3.3 kV SiC (TI)
Two-channel half-bridge driver template: UCC218915-Q1 pre-drivers (2.8 A split outputs, DESAT, soft shutdown, CLMPE clamp, ASC) into discrete P/N-MOSFET buffers, each channel with a UCC35131-Q1 isolated bias module (+18/-5 V from 12 V). TI: design not warranted production-worthy (schematic notice).
- **Native ratings** (TIDUFH7 Mar 2026 p.2-6; SLVRC10 Rev E1 p.1-2): FETs up to 3.3 kV when the MCU is referenced to the DC-link midpoint; parts rated 1050 Vrms working; UCC35131 2.0 W at 85 degC, VIN 5.5-20 V, > 5 kVrms, 8.2 mm creepage/clearance; double-pulse test on a 2.3 kV / 1.1 mohm module at 1.5 kV, 400 A off / 320 A on, 2.5 ohm: about 40 V/ns off, 50 V/ns on.
- **Informs:** DAB gate-drive strength vs RG_off (buffer stage), Miller clamp topology (D-028), bias module and barrier options (D-016), insulation coordination (ECO-10), driver selection under D-031.
- **Limitation:** no DESAT threshold, blanking, short-circuit or fault-timing data; the pre-driver in the BOM is pre-production (PUCC218915AQDFPQ1). Doc inconsistencies: turn-off CMTI printed as 40nV/ns for about 40 V/ns (p.5); test text says 2.5 ohm on and off, the BOM fits 2 x 3.3 ohm on and 2 x 1 ohm off per channel. The 12 V bias input does not fit our 24 V gate-drive rail.
- **What we take from it:** a discrete buffer lets a 2.8 A pre-driver drive the gate (would let the DAB drop its 0.27 ohm RG_off), and the N-FET clamp from CLMPE (detail in docs/requirements/REFERENCE-LESSONS.md).
- **Files:** design guide TIDUFH7, schematic SLVRC10 (Rev E1), BOM SLURBB9 (Rev E1). Not fetched: PCB layout and CAD files (layout out of scope), UCC35131-Q1-CALC tool.
